Privacy policy – Minyan Board

The Minyan Board website and app do not collect, store or share any personal information. They download the shul's public prayer schedule and compute the times on your device. They use no analytics, no advertising and no accounts, and they do not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Text messages (SMS)

If you text a keyword to the shul's number, we receive your phone number and the text of your message and use them only to send you the automated reply you requested. We do not send recurring or promotional messages. Your phone number and your consent to receive text messages are not sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help.

Questions: contact the shul.
