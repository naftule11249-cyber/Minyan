Privacy policy – Cascade Zmanim

Cascade Zmanim is a privately run minyan-times service and is not an official publication of any synagogue. The website and the app do not collect, store or share any personal information. They download a public schedule file and compute the times on your device. They use no analytics, no advertising and no accounts, and they do not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Reminders (app notifications)

If you turn on reminders, the app stores on our reminder service only the technical notification address your phone provides and the minyanim and times you chose, so that it can send you those reminders. Turning reminders off deletes them.

Text messages (SMS)

If you text a keyword to the Cascade Zmanim number, we receive your phone number and the text of your message and use them only to send you the automated reply you requested, or the reminders you asked for by text. We do not send promotional messages. Your phone number and your consent to receive text messages are not sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time; reply HELP for help.

Questions: reply to any message from the service, or text HELP to the number.
